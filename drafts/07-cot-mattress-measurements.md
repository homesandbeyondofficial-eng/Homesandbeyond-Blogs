# Cot Mattress Measurements: The Details to Confirm Before Ordering Fitted Sheets

**Status: DRAFT — NOT APPROVED FOR PUBLICATION**

A fitted sheet that looks like it should fit can still be too loose, too short or difficult to tuck around the mattress. Cot mattresses are not always identical, so checking the actual mattress dimensions before ordering saves guesswork.

Take a few minutes to measure the mattress itself rather than relying only on the cot's product name or the size mentioned on an old packaging label.

## Measure length, width and depth

Use a tape measure and note three dimensions:

- **Length:** Measure from one end of the mattress to the other.
- **Width:** Measure across the mattress at its widest point.
- **Depth:** Measure from the top surface down to the bottom edge.

Measure the mattress without compressing it. If it has rounded corners, note that too. The fitted sheet needs to be designed for the mattress shape and dimensions, not just the cot frame.

## Why depth is easy to overlook

A sheet may match the length and width but have pockets that are not deep enough. If the mattress is thicker than the sheet's pocket, the corners can pop off. Conversely, a pocket that is much deeper than the mattress may leave excess fabric.

Check the fitted-sheet depth or mattress-height range listed by the seller. Also look for elastic around the corners or perimeter and follow the product's fit guidance.

## Confirm the mattress and sheet combination

Before placing an order, compare:
1. The exact mattress length and width
2. Mattress depth
3. The sheet's stated size and pocket depth
4. Any corner shape or mattress topper you use
5. The product's care and fit instructions

If you're comparing elastic styles, our article on [fitted-sheet depth and elastic](https://homesnbeyond.in/blogs/blogs/fitted-sheet-depth-and-elastic-two-details-that-affect-cot-mattress-fit) goes into why both details affect the finished fit.

## Do not size by cot frame alone

The outside measurements of a cot frame are not the same as the sleeping surface. A cot labelled with a standard size may still have a mattress with slightly different dimensions. Always use the mattress measurements as your reference.

For babies, follow current safe-sleep guidance and use a fitted sheet that is designed to fit the mattress snugly. Do not add loose layers or padding to make an ill-fitting sheet work. If you cannot get a secure fit, choose a sheet that matches the mattress or contact the manufacturer for advice.

## Keep your measurements handy

Save the three measurements in your phone or write them on a note in the nursery. They will help when you buy replacement sheets, compare fabrics or order a spare set later.

A little measuring before shopping makes it easier to choose a fitted sheet that stays in place as intended and makes laundry-day changes less frustrating.
